Local Notes

Condos, townhomes and waterfront-area homes

If you own a condo or townhome in West Palm Beach, your building or association will usually have rules about what work is allowed, which hours contractors can work, and what approvals or paperwork are required before demolition starts. These rules vary a lot from one property to the next, so we ask about them at the very first conversation.

Homes closer to the water deal with salt in the air, which is tough on exterior paint, metal fixtures and hardware. Choosing the right coating and keeping up with prep makes a visible difference in how long a finish lasts.

No matter the property type, we aim to give you a written scope you can compare, so you know what is included before work begins.
