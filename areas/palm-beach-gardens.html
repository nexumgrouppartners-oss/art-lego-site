Local Notes

Painting in an HOA community

Before an exterior repaint in an HOA community, it is common to submit your color choices and sometimes product details to the association for approval. Approved palettes, sheen rules and review turnaround times differ from one community to the next.

Our habit is to settle the colors and get approval first, then schedule the painting. That keeps the crew from standing idle and keeps you from repainting a wall the association has not signed off on.

For golf and gated communities especially, we also coordinate around gate access, work hours and any vendor paperwork the community requires. Tell us about these requirements when you call and we will build them into the plan.
