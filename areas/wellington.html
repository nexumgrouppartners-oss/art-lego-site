Local Notes

Remodeling in a Wellington community

A large share of Wellington homes sit inside homeowners associations. For interior work such as a kitchen or bathroom, an association usually does not need to weigh in. Exterior changes are different. New paint colors, windows, doors or anything visible from the street often need architectural review first, and some communities have their own timelines and forms.

We tell you up front if your project is likely to need approval, and we plan the schedule around it so you are not stuck waiting with a half-finished job. If you are not sure whether your community requires sign-off, your association or management company can confirm, and we can help you prepare the request.

Because we are based here, we also understand the practical side of Wellington living: summer storms, humidity that is hard on paint and bathrooms, and a hurricane season that affects when exterior work is smart to schedule.
