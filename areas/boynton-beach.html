Local Notes

Why moisture decides the bathroom plan

In South Florida a bathroom works hard. Steam, warm temperatures and slow-drying air mean water protection behind the tile matters as much as the tile itself. When we open up a shower, we look at the waterproofing, the substrate and the ventilation, because a pretty finish over a poor base will not last.

The walls and ceiling need the right paint too. A standard flat finish in a bathroom can peel or mildew, so we choose products intended for damp rooms.

If you are closer to the coast in eastern Boynton Beach, salt air can also affect exterior paint and metal hardware. We take that into account when we recommend materials and finishes.
