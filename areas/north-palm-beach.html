Local Notes

Building and painting near the water

Salt air does slow damage. Unprotected metal rusts faster, caulk and sealant break down sooner, and paint that was fine inland can chalk or peel. The cure is mostly in the preparation: cleaning off salt and mildew, repairing bare spots, sealing joints and using a coating system suited to the exposure.

Timing matters too. Exterior work is easiest to plan outside the peak of hurricane season, and it is smart to have your home checked before a storm threatens instead of after. If your exterior needs attention, talk with us early so the job can be scheduled with the weather in mind.

Inside, the same humidity that rusts hardware outdoors puts pressure on kitchens and bathrooms, so we favor materials made to handle moisture.
