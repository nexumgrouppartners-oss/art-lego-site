How to Hire a Painting Contractor in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

By Art Lego LLC · South Florida · October 30, 2026

If you want to hire a painter in Palm Beach Gardens, start with a written estimate that lists the full scope. It should cover the prep work, the paint, and the number of coats. Ask every painter for the same details so you can compare bids fairly.

Why the Estimate Matters More Than the Price

Two painting bids can look far apart and still cover different work. One may include washing walls, filling holes, and two coats of primer. The other may only list two coats of paint. Read each estimate line by line before you decide.

A clear estimate protects you. It gives you a fixed list of what will happen in your home. It also gives you something to check if questions come up later.

What a Good Painting Estimate Should List

Look for these details in writing:

  • The rooms or exterior areas included, with a room-by-room or wall-by-wall breakdown.
  • Surface prep, such as cleaning, scraping, sanding, caulking, and patching nail holes or cracks.
  • Primer, including where it will be used and why.
  • The paint brand, product line, and sheen for each area, such as flat, eggshell, satin, or semi-gloss.
  • The number of coats for walls, ceilings, trim, and doors.
  • Protection for floors, furniture, landscaping, and fixtures during the work.
  • Cleanup at the end, including removing tape, drop cloths, and debris.
  • A start date and a rough timeline.

If a line item is missing, ask about it. A good painter will answer plainly and update the estimate if needed.

Questions to Ask Before You Sign

Before you choose a painter, ask these questions. Write down the answers.

  • Who will be on the job, and will the same people work the whole time?
  • Do you do your own prep work, or do you hire it out?
  • What paint will you use, and can I see the product name?
  • How do you handle surprises, such as rot, peeling paint, or damaged drywall?
  • How will you protect my belongings and keep the work area clean each day?
  • Who is my point of contact if I have a question during the project?

Pay attention to how the painter answers. Clear, direct answers usually mean the work has been planned well.

How to Compare Scope Between Bids

Put the estimates side by side. Check each one against the same list: rooms, prep, primer, paint, coats, and cleanup. If one bid leaves out prep, the price may look lower only because the job is smaller.

Also compare the timeline. Ask how many days the work will take and whether you need to move furniture before the work starts. A painter who plans the job room by room can often keep your home usable while the work moves along.

Do not choose based on one number alone. Choose the painter who explains the work well and gives you a clear plan you can follow.

Interior vs. Exterior Painting Estimates

Interior and exterior bids need different details. Interior work often focuses on walls, ceilings, trim, cabinets, and doors. Exterior work also covers siding, soffits, fascia, shutters, and stucco areas, and it depends heavily on the weather. Ask how the painter plans for heat, humidity, and afternoon storms, which are common in Palm Beach County.

Cabinet and trim painting needs extra care. Ask about sanding, the primer used on wood, and how many coats are planned. Smooth results on cabinets take more time than many people expect.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • A verbal-only quote with no written scope.
  • Pressure to sign the same day.
  • No questions about the surfaces, colors, or prep needs.
  • Vague paint descriptions, such as good paint with no product name.
  • Large deposit requests before any work begins.

If you see these signs, slow down. Ask for a written estimate and take time to compare.

Planning Your Painting Project in Palm Beach Gardens

Homes in Palm Beach Gardens come in many styles. Some have single-story layouts, and others have two stories with large trim and high ceilings. Tell your painter about the age of the home, the surfaces you want painted, and any problem areas you have noticed. Older walls may need more patching, and exterior paint may need more scraping before new coats go on.

Think about colors before the work starts. Bring paint samples and look at them in morning and evening light. A small test patch on a wall can save time later.

Plan for the day the work starts, too. Move photos and wall decor, clear shelves near the work area, and let everyone in the home know when the painter will be there. Ask how the painter will handle pets, children, and daily access to bathrooms and kitchens. Keep a copy of the signed estimate with your project notes so you can check the work against it later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should a painting estimate cost in Palm Beach Gardens?

Pricing depends on the size of the job, the prep work, the paint, and how easy the surfaces are to reach. Ask each painter for a written estimate with the same scope so you can compare totals fairly.

Do I need to move furniture before painting?

Move small items and decor out of the work area. Ask the painter what they will move and protect, and what you should clear before the work starts.

Why do some painters ask for primer?

Primer helps paint stick, blocks stains, and evens out color on new drywall, patched areas, and bare wood. Ask where the painter plans to use it and why.
