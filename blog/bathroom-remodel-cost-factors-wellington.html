Bathroom Remodel Cost Factors in Wellington, FL

By Art Lego LLC · South Florida · September 28, 2026

Bathroom remodel costs in Wellington, FL depend mainly on scope, tile and fixture choices, and whether plumbing gets moved. A cosmetic refresh with new fixtures and a fresh coat of paint costs far less than a full gut renovation that repositions the shower, tub, or vanity. Here’s what actually drives the price.

What Determines the Cost of a Bathroom Remodel?

Scope is the starting point for every bathroom remodel budget. Swapping a faucet, repainting, and updating hardware is a different project entirely from gutting a bathroom down to the studs and reconfiguring the layout. Before you call for quotes, get clear on what you actually want to change—cosmetic updates, a full renovation, or something in between—since that decision affects every number that follows.

How Much Does Tile Choice Affect Bathroom Remodel Pricing?

Tile is one of the most visible cost swings in a bathroom remodel. Basic ceramic tile in a simple layout costs less than natural stone, large-format porcelain, or intricate mosaic patterns that take longer to install. Shower niches, accent bands, and floor-to-ceiling tile in a walk-in shower all add labor time on top of material cost, so it’s worth deciding early how much of the bathroom you want tiled.

Does Moving the Shower or Tub Change the Budget?

Yes—relocating a shower, tub, toilet, or sink means new rough-in plumbing behind the walls and under the floor, which adds licensed trade labor that a like-for-like fixture swap doesn’t require. Keeping fixtures in their existing footprint and upgrading finishes in place is consistently one of the most effective ways to control cost, while a full layout change gives you more design freedom at a higher price point.

What Do Vanities and Fixtures Cost in a Bathroom Remodel?

Vanities range from refacing an existing cabinet with a new countertop and hardware, to a full custom vanity built for the space. Faucets, showerheads, and hardware finishes also span a wide range depending on brand and material. Refacing is typically the more budget-friendly path when the existing vanity is structurally sound and the right size for your layout; full replacement makes more sense when it isn’t.

How Does a Full Gut Renovation Compare to a Refresh?

A full gut renovation—new plumbing, new electrical, a reconfigured layout, and all-new finishes—takes longer and costs more than a refresh that keeps the existing footprint and swaps out surfaces and fixtures. Water damage, outdated plumbing, or a layout that no longer works for your household are the usual reasons homeowners choose a full renovation over a lighter refresh.

Do Wellington HOA Rules Affect Bathroom Remodel Costs?

Most bathroom remodels are entirely interior and don’t require HOA review, but if your project touches plumbing vents, exterior walls, or anything visible from outside the home, some Wellington-area HOAs require sign-off before work begins. That review doesn’t typically add material cost on its own, but skipping it can lead to costly delays or rework if the association flags something after the fact—checking your community’s guidelines early is worth the time.

What We Recommend When Budgeting Your Bathroom Remodel

Start by separating must-haves from nice-to-haves, then decide whether you’re after a cosmetic refresh or a full renovation—that single decision shapes most of the budget. If cost control matters most, keep plumbing fixtures in their current locations and focus your budget on tile, vanity, and fixture finishes rather than a layout change. And if your home is in a Wellington-area HOA community, check the guidelines before finalizing your scope so the plan you budget for is the plan you can actually build. From there, a licensed contractor walking your space in person will give you a far more accurate number than any general estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an average bathroom remodel cost in Wellington, FL?
It depends heavily on scope: a cosmetic refresh with new fixtures and paint costs far less than a full gut renovation that moves plumbing and reconfigures the layout. Because every bathroom and every homeowner's wish list is different, the most accurate way to get a number is a free, in-home estimate rather than a generic figure.

Is it cheaper to reface a vanity than to replace it?
Generally yes. Refacing or repainting an existing vanity and swapping the countertop and hardware costs less than a full custom vanity replacement, since it reuses the existing cabinet box and doesn't require new plumbing rough-in. A full replacement makes more sense when the existing vanity is damaged, undersized, or simply doesn't fit the new layout.

Does adding a walk-in shower cost more than keeping a tub?
Converting a tub to a walk-in shower typically costs more than a like-for-like tub replacement, since it usually involves new waterproofing, a curb or curbless pan, and sometimes plumbing relocation. It's a popular upgrade for accessibility and modern style, but it's worth budgeting for the extra waterproofing and framing work up front.

How much do HOA requirements add to a bathroom remodel budget in Wellington?
Most bathroom remodels are entirely interior and don't need HOA sign-off, but if the project touches plumbing vents, exterior walls, or anything visible from outside, some Wellington-area HOAs require review before work starts. That review itself doesn't usually add material cost, but skipping it can add costly delays if work has to pause for approval.

What's the biggest cost driver in a bathroom remodel?
Whether plumbing fixtures move is usually the single biggest driver, since relocating a shower, tub, toilet, or sink means new rough-in plumbing behind the walls and floor. Keeping fixtures in their existing locations and upgrading finishes in place is consistently the fastest way to control a bathroom remodel budget.

Every bathroom is different, and the most reliable way to budget yours is to walk through your goals with a licensed contractor.
