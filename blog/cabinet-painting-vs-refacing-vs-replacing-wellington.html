Cabinet Painting vs. Refacing vs. Replacing in Wellington, FL

By Art Lego LLC · Wellington, FL · October 26, 2026

Painting keeps your existing cabinet boxes and doors and changes only the finish. Refacing keeps the boxes but replaces doors, drawer fronts and surface veneer. Replacing removes everything and installs new cabinets. In Wellington, FL, painting is the lowest-cost route when the boxes are sound, and replacement is right when the layout or the boxes themselves are failing.

Cabinets set both the look and much of the cost of a kitchen, so it pays to pick the right level of work. Here is how the three options compare for homes in Wellington and Palm Beach County.

What is the difference between painting, refacing and replacing kitchen cabinets?

Painting (or refinishing) cleans, sands, primes and coats the existing doors, drawer fronts and boxes. Nothing is removed except hardware and doors for finishing. Refacing keeps the cabinet boxes in place, covers their visible faces with new veneer or laminate, and installs new doors, drawer fronts and hardware. Replacing removes the old cabinets completely and installs new ones, which is the only option that lets you change the layout.

All three can make a kitchen look new. They differ in how much disruption they cause, how much they change the layout, and how much of the old structure you keep.

When is cabinet painting the right call?

Painting fits when the boxes are solid, square and dry, the layout works, and the main complaint is color or a worn finish. It is typically the lowest-cost update and the fastest, and it is a good way to change the style of a kitchen without a full remodel. It does not change door style, fix damaged boxes, or add storage.

Preparation decides how long a painted finish lasts. Cleaning off kitchen grease, sanding, priming with the right primer and using a durable cabinet-grade coating all matter. See our guide to interior painting and Florida humidity for how moisture affects prep and dry time.

When does cabinet refacing make sense?

Refacing sits between paint and replacement. It makes sense when the boxes are in good shape and the layout works, but you want a different door style or a new surface that paint cannot give you. Because the boxes stay, there is no demolition of the cabinet run and less disruption than a full replacement. It cannot fix swollen or water-damaged boxes, and it cannot change the layout.

When should you replace the cabinets?

Replace when the layout needs to change, when you want a different storage plan, or when the boxes are failing. In Palm Beach County humidity, particleboard boxes near the sink and dishwasher can swell and separate, and no paint or veneer will hold on a box that is coming apart. Replacement also makes sense when you are already moving plumbing, electrical or appliances as part of a larger remodel. Is replacing kitchen cabinets hard to do yourself?

It is harder than it looks. Old cabinets must be removed without damaging walls, new boxes must be leveled across uneven floors and walls, and countertops, plumbing and electrical often need to be disconnected and reconnected by licensed trades. Mistakes show up as gaps, doors that do not line up, and countertops that do not fit. Painting is the option homeowners most often attempt themselves, but a professional-looking result still depends on careful prep.

Which option is the best way to update a kitchen on a budget?

Painting is usually the least expensive way to change a kitchen, followed by refacing, with replacement the largest project. Keeping the existing layout keeps costs down in every case, because it avoids moving plumbing and electrical work. What we recommend

If they are damaged or the layout does not work for your household, plan a replacement. Get a written scope from a licensed contractor so you can compare the options on the same terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you paint laminate or thermofoil cabinets?
Sometimes. Laminate usually holds paint if it is cleaned, sanded and primed with a bonding primer. Thermofoil that is peeling or bubbling usually has to be replaced, because paint will not fix a failing surface.

How do painted cabinets hold up in Florida humidity?
Durability depends mostly on preparation and the coating used. Thorough cleaning, sanding, a bonding primer and a durable cabinet-grade finish matter more than the color, and sound, dry cabinet boxes are the starting point.

Is refacing worth it compared with replacing?
Refacing makes sense when the boxes are sound and the layout works but the doors and drawer fronts look dated. If the boxes are damaged or the layout needs to change, replacing is usually the better investment.

Do I need approval to change my kitchen cabinets in Wellington?
Cosmetic cabinet work often needs none, but moving plumbing or electrical can. Check your HOA rules and confirm permit requirements with the Village of Wellington before work starts.
